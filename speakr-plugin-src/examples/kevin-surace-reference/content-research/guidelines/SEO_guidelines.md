# SEO Guidelines: Kevin Surace Blog

**MANDATORY READING: Every blog post must follow these SEO guidelines tailored for Kevin Surace's thought leadership blog at kevinsurace.com.**

---

## Primary Distribution

- **Blog home:** kevinsurace.com (blog section)
- **Primary distribution:** LinkedIn (articles and post links)
- **Secondary:** Organic search, speaker bureau discovery, podcast show notes

---

## Keyword Strategy

**Cluster 1: AI Leadership**
- ai leadership, ai transformation, leading through ai, ai adoption strategy

**Cluster 2: Innovation & Invention**
- ai inventor, ai virtual assistant history, technology innovation
- serial entrepreneur ai, patents innovation, building ai products
- ai pioneer, first ai assistant, ai history

**Cluster 3: Joy-Success Cycle / Human Performance**
- joy and success, curiosity in business, purpose-driven leadership
- sustainable success, joy-success cycle, peak performance leadership
- curiosity as business strategy, avoiding burnout leadership

**Cluster 4: Keynote / Thought Leadership**
- ai keynote speaker, technology keynote, innovation speaker
- ai thought leader, future of work speaker, enterprise ai expert

### Keyword Integration Rules
- **Natural placement**: Keywords must flow within Kevin's conversational voice — never force keywords into awkward phrasing
- **Semantic variations**: Use related terms and synonyms generously
- **Strategic density**: 1.5-2.5% keyword density for primary keyword
- **Title optimization**: Include primary keyword in H1 and within first 100 words
- **Conclusion anchor**: Primary keyword should appear naturally in the closing section

## Meta Elements

### Title Tags (50-60 characters)
**Patterns:**
- `[Topic]: [Kevin's Perspective/Reframe]`
- `Why [Conventional Wisdom] Is Wrong About [Topic]`
- `[Topic] — What [X] Years of Building AI Taught Me`

**Examples:**
- "AI Won't Replace Leaders — But It Will Expose Bad Ones"
- "Curiosity Is a Skill, Not a Trait: Here's How to Build It"
- "The Joy-Success Cycle: Why Joy Comes Before Achievement"
- "What 95 Patents Taught Me About Innovation"

### Meta Descriptions (150-160 characters)
**Pattern:** `[Hook/reframe]. Kevin Surace — AI inventor, 95 patents — shares [what]. [Benefit to reader].`

**Examples:**
- "Success doesn't create joy. Joy creates success. Kevin Surace — AI inventor and 95-patent holder — shares why curiosity is your most valuable leadership skill."
- "AI is changing every leadership role. Kevin Surace, who invented the first AI assistant, explains what actually matters now — and what doesn't."

---

## Header Hierarchy

- **H1**: One per page, includes primary keyword, written as a compelling statement or reframe (not a generic label)
- **H2**: 5-8 per post, major sections — use keyword variations, written conversationally
- **H3**: 8+ per post, subsections — use long-tail keywords, can be questions or short phrases
- **H4**: Rare — only for nested detail within H3 sections

### H2 Style Guide (Kevin's Voice)
**Good H2s (sound like Kevin):**
- "The Pattern I Keep Seeing"
- "Why Most Leaders Get This Wrong"
- "What AI Actually Changes — and What It Doesn't"
- "Curiosity Isn't Optional Anymore"

**Bad H2s (too generic/corporate):**
- "Understanding AI Transformation"
- "Key Benefits of AI Adoption"
- "The Importance of Leadership in the Digital Age"

## Internal Linking Strategy

### Priority Link Targets (Min 2 Per Post)
1. **Kevin's speaking page** — link when mentioning keynotes, events, or "I talk about this on stage"
2. **Book page (*The Joy-Success Cycle*)** — link when referencing the book, joy-success framework, or curiosity methodology
3. **Other blog posts** — cross-link between posts in the same topic cluster
4. **Podcast/media appearances** — link when referencing interviews or media features

### Link Anchor Text Rules
- Use natural, conversational anchor text — never "click here"
- Weave links into Kevin's voice: "I wrote about this in *The Joy-Success Cycle*" or "When I keynote on this topic..."
- Vary anchor text across posts (don't repeat exact same phrases)

---

## External Citation Rules

- **Max 3 external citations per post**
- **Tier 1 sources preferred:** McKinsey, Gartner, Forrester, Harvard Business Review, MIT Sloan, Stanford HAI, World Economic Forum
- **Citation format:** Inline `(Source: Domain Name)` — no full URLs in body text
- **Kevin's experience as primary evidence:** Use phrases like "At Appvance, we've found..." or "When I was building the AI assistant at General Magic..."
- **NEVER use named third-party case studies** — use Kevin's own experiences, anonymized examples, or published research

## Content Differentiation

### How Kevin's Posts Should Differ from Competitors

**vs. Generic AI blogs:** Kevin's posts lead with personal invention stories and 95-patent credibility — not trend aggregation or tool listicles.

**vs. Corporate thought leadership:** Kevin uses conversational voice, rhetorical questions, sentence fragments, and humor — not formal business prose.

**vs. Self-help content:** Kevin grounds joy/curiosity content in neuroscience, business data, and his own company-building experience — not abstract motivation.

**The SEO angle:** Kevin's posts should rank for queries where readers want the perspective of someone who has *actually built* the technology, not just someone commenting on it. Optimize for intent signals like "how to," "why," "what actually," and "real experience."
